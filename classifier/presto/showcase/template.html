Silage or grain corn? · Beltsville Agricultural Research Center, Maryland · test years 2021–2023

Presto judges every 10 m pixel, then the field takes the vote

Each field below was scored once by a model that never saw its label. Switch between what the satellite recorded each month and what Presto concluded, pixel by pixel. Click a field to see its year.

1One pixel, twelve monthsFor every 10 m pixel: Sentinel-2's 10 bands (visible red, green, blue plus red-edge, near- and shortwave-infrared), NDVI and Sentinel-1 radar, one value per month (for Sentinel-2, the median of clear readings).
2A pretrained readerPresto, a transformer NASA Harvest pretrained on unlabeled pixel time series from around the world, turns each pixel's year into 128 numbers.
3Fine-tuned on our labelsThe whole model is trained further on the 81 labeled field-years from 2017–2020, 64 random pixels per field-year per epoch, each carrying its field's label. Settings were chosen by holding out one year at a time.
4Pixels vote, the field decidesEvery pixel gets a silage score from 0 to 1. A field's score is the mean over its pixels and five training runs; 0.5 or more means silage.

How good is it, honestly?

Official test (train on 2017–2020, score the 44 labeled 2021–2023 field-years once): fine-tuned Presto 41 of 44 right, the simple 5-feature model 39 of 44. Presto is right on 4 field-years where the simple model is wrong and wrong on 2 where it is right, so the difference is not significant (McNemar p = 0.69). On five mixed-year splits the simple model leads, 96 to 92 of 101.

What it seems to pick up: silage is cut green in late summer and the field goes bare; grain stands and dries into October or November. Silage fields here also green up again in November and December, probably a cover crop: hiding those two months drops silage caught from 11 to 8 of 12.

Limits: one farm, 44 test field-years; each one is 2.3 points of accuracy.

Data: Sentinel-2 L2A and Sentinel-1 RTC from Microsoft Planetary Computer; Presto (nasaharvest/presto, MIT). True color is red, green and blue reflectance as Presto received it. Pixels are shrunk 10 m inside each field outline.
